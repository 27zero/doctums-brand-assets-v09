The Golden Canon Grid — Doctums' signature structural device. Draws golden-section vertical/horizontal guides and canon diagonals over a positioned parent, expressing the "mathematical rigor / architectural precision" the brand is built on. Decorative guide layer; use behind hero compositions or in brand specimens.

```jsx
<div style={{ position: 'relative', aspectRatio: '16/9' }}>
  <GoldenCanonGrid color="var(--accent)" opacity={0.18} />
  {/* content anchored to the intersections */}
</div>
```
