Brand image frame — applies the signature Doctums photographic treatment: warm desaturated grade plus the golden→cyan gradient overlay at ~30%. Use for all editorial/campus imagery so photos read as one family.

```jsx
<PhotoFrame src="../../assets/imagery/architecture-07.png" alt="Campus facade" caption="Campus architecture" />
<PhotoFrame src="../../assets/imagery/practitioners-06.png" ratio="4 / 5" overlay={false} />
```

Four official directions in `assets/imagery/`:
- `architecture-01` to `23` — bridges, concrete structures, glass façades
- `movement-01` to `13` — motion-blur crowds, campus plazas, stairs
- `students-01` to `13` — corridors, interiors, enrollment scenes
- `practitioners-01` to `20` — teams with plans, screens, data-ops
